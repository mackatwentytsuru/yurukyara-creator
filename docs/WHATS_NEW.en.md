# What's new

What each new version lets you do, newest first, one line each.
How to update: [README, "Updating"](../README.en.md#updating). Your characters, personalities, voices, memories and settings are kept.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-08)

- Talk with Norrie. Switch between 3D and 2D with a button.
- Without any key, characters talk with text and retro sounds.
- Characters remember what you talked about. Say 「わすれて」 ("forget it") and everything is erased.
- Carry a conversation to another browser tab with a 6-digit number.
- Pick the personality, way of talking, energy, reply length and voice role (auto, boy or girl) on screen.
- On your own PC with Claude Code, make your own yuru-chara picture from a few words or one picture, and ask for fixes in a few words.
- Say "update" (「アップデートして」) to get the new version without losing your characters, and go back to the previous version if you want.
